Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ToolBharat ("hum", "hamari website") aapki privacy ka poora dhyan rakhti hai. Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ki hum kaunsi jaankari collect karte hain, kaise use karte hain, aur aapki suraksha kaise karte hain.

1. Kaunsi Jaankari Collect Karte Hain

Hum minimum data collect karte hain:

  • Search queries — Jo IFSC/MICR codes aap search karte hain (localStorage mein save hote hain, hamare server par nahi)
  • Browser information — Anonymous analytics (page views, country, device type)
  • Cookies — Basic functionality ke liye (aapki search history yaad rakhne ke liye)

2. Kaunsi Jaankari Collect Nahi Karte

  • ❌ Aapka naam, email, phone number
  • ❌ Aapki bank account details
  • ❌ Aapke financial transactions
  • ❌ Aapki location (GPS)

3. Jaankari Kaise Use Karte Hain

  • Website improve karne ke liye
  • Aapki search history yaad rakhne ke liye (browser mein)
  • Anonymous traffic analysis ke liye

4. Cookies

Hamari website cookies use karti hai taaki:

  • Aapki recent searches yaad rahe
  • Website faster load ho
  • Anonymous analytics mil sake

Aap browser settings se cookies disable kar sakte hain, lekin isse kuch features kaam nahi karenge.

5. Third-Party Services

Hum ye services use karte hain:

  • Cloudflare — Website hosting aur security
  • Google Analytics (future) — Anonymous traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense (future) — Advertisements

In services ki apni privacy policies hoti hain. Hum aapko unhe padhne ki salah dete hain.

6. Data Security

Hum aapke data ki suraksha ke liye HTTPS encryption use karte hain. Aapki search history sirf aapke browser mein save hoti hai, hamare server par nahi.

7. Bachchon Ki Privacy

Hamari website 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon ke liye nahi hai. Hum jaan-boojh kar bachchon ka data collect nahi karte.

8. Aapke Adhikaar

Aap ye kar sakte hain:

  • Apni search history clear kar sakte hain (browser se)
  • Cookies disable kar sakte hain
  • Humse data deletion request kar sakte hain

9. Privacy Policy Mein Badlav

Hum ye policy kabhi bhi update kar sakte hain. Latest version hamesha is page par milega.

10. Contact

Privacy ke baare mein koi sawaal ho toh humein email karein:
