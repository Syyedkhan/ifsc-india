About ToolBharat

India's trusted IFSC code lookup platform

ToolBharat ek free online platform hai jo Indian users ko bank branch ki sahi jaankari pradan karta hai. Hamari website ke through aap kisi bhi bank branch ka IFSC code, MICR code, SWIFT code, pura pata, contact number, aur NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/UPI suvidha ki jaankari le sakte hain.

Hamara maksad hai ki India ke har kone tak banking information aasan bhasha mein pahunchaye. Chahe aap kisi chhote kasbe se ho ya bade shehar se, aapko sahi jaankari milni chahiye — bina kisi paid subscription ya login ke.

Hamara Mission

India mein har din karodon log online paisa transfer karte hain. Lekin sahi IFSC code dhoondhna aaj bhi mushkil hai — kuch websites purani hain, kuch ad se bhari hain, aur kuch mein data sahi nahi hota.

ToolBharat ka mission simple hai:

  • Accuracy — Har IFSC code ko verify karke daalte hain
  • Speed — Instant search, koi waiting nahi
  • Simplicity — Aasan interface, har koi use kar sake
  • Free — Hamesha free, koi hidden charge nahi
  • Privacy — Aapka data safe, koi tracking nahi

Data Source

Hamari jaankari in official sources se li jaati hai:

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — Official IFSC and MICR codes
  • Bank ki official websites — Branch address, contact, services
  • Publicly available IFSC databases — Verified and cross-checked

Hum regularly data update karte hain taaki aapko sabse sahi jaankari mile. Agar aapko koi galat jaankari dikhe, toh humein zaroor batayein — hum turant theek karenge.
